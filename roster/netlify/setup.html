Turn on your live class

The page is online. The live class also needs its small server, which comes in the same zip. Without it, only the demo class works. Switching it on takes about 3 minutes and no code.

Checking the class server…

1. Drop it again, logged in

Log in first. Without an account, Netlify publishes the page but not the server.

  1. Go to app.netlify.com and log in. If you don't have an account, sign up free with your email or GitHub.
  2. On your computer, double-click roster-netlify.zip. You get a folder called roster-netlify.
  3. In Netlify, open Projects. At the bottom is a box that says you can drag and drop your project folder. Drag the roster-netlify folder onto it.
  4. Wait until it says Published, then open the link Netlify gives you.

It worked if the first screen says "Your class is live. You're the first one here." This page also turns green.

2. Still demo only? Connect GitHub instead

This always sets up the server.

  1. In Netlify, click Add new project, then Import an existing project, then GitHub. Let Netlify see your repositories.
  2. Pick the es30 repository.
  3. Set Branch to deploy to claude/vibe-coding-mvps-twhakh.
  4. Set Base directory to roster/netlify. Leave everything else as it is.
  5. Click Deploy, wait about a minute, then open the link.

Once it's live

  1. Open the site yourself before you share it. Click I'm running this class. The first person to click it becomes the organizer.
  2. Finish the short setup. STAT 110's pset dates and the two experiment cycles are filled in for you.
  3. Your home screen shows a checklist:
    • Paste your students' names to get a personal link for each one.
    • Text or email each student their own link.
    • Save your organizer link.
  4. For your submission and your TF, use the demo link your-site.netlify.app/?demo. It's a copy with example classmates that anyone can try without joining your real class.
